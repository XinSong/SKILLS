# Chinese classroom-note style

Read this file before drafting or editing a Chinese classroom note.

## Goal

Write compact technical Chinese that is easy to publish and revisit while
remaining faithful to the course sequence and prepared evidence.

## Expression

- State definitions, claims, reasoning, examples, procedures, caveats, and
  results directly.
- Prefer short paragraphs and information-dense lists. Allocate length by
  knowledge density rather than a fixed bullet count.
- Use one Chinese rendering for each technical term. Retain the English term on
  first occurrence when it aids recognition.
- Preserve attribution only when the named source changes the epistemic meaning
  of a claim.
- Preserve uncertainty and modal force. Never strengthen a tentative claim.
- Remove medium narration such as “讲者指出”, “课件展示”, “视频中提到”, and
  their English equivalents.
- Remove generic transitions, repeated conclusions, and model commentary.
- Do not add external explanations or reorganize the course by concept.

## Editing boundary

The final editing pass may improve terminology, cohesion, and concision. It may
not reorder course sections, remove an important knowledge unit, add unsupported
claims, or move all slides into a separate gallery.
